Our Story

STAR E-mitra was founded with a single mission: to make digital and government services accessible to everyone in Rajasthan. What started as a small cyber cafe has evolved into a comprehensive digital service hub trusted by thousands of customers.

Shekh Firoz, the founder and operator, brings years of dedicated experience in providing seamless government service solutions, banking transactions, and professional cyber cafe services. With a commitment to excellence, speed, and customer satisfaction, STAR E-mitra has become a household name for reliable digital services.

Our cyber cafe facility includes high-speed internet, printing, scanning, and document services. We serve as a government authorized point for E-Mitra services, processing applications for Aadhar, PAN, Jan Aadhar, and various certificates in record time.
